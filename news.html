    Preprint: full-field DeepONet for structural dynamics

    Submitted “A Full-field Extended Deep Operator Network as a Spatio-temporal Surrogate for Structural Dynamics” to Engineering Structures; preprint posted on arXiv. The paper introduces a DeepONet variant that returns multiple full-field dynamical outputs in a single pass and benchmarks it on a cable-stayed bridge model.
